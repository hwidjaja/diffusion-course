Course Modules

Module & Title Topics Covered Key Outcomes
0: Orientation Why Learn Diffusion This Way? • Philosophy of the course • The two-pillar framework • What this course covers (and doesn’t) Understand the course's unique approach and structure
1: What Makes Diffusion Different Why not GANs or VAEs? • Diffusion as a process • Stochastic inversion Grasp why diffusion is a paradigm shift in generative modeling
2: The First Pillar – Denoising Adding noise • The denoising objective (DDPM) • Conditional vs marginal scores • Visual intuition Learn how denoising is a proxy for the data's gradient field
3: Deep Dive – Score Matching Equivalence Deriving the score matching target • Posterior-weighted averages • Why denoising equals score matching • MSE's role Understand the theoretical link between denoising and score matching
4: The Second Pillar – Sampling as Physics Langevin dynamics • Generation as "rolling a die" • Connection to SDEs • Inverse problems via noise reversal See sampling not as a trick, but as a simulated physical process
5: Connecting It All The full generative loop • How the pillars reinforce each other • Architecture-agnostic benefits • Practical implications Synthesize the two pillars into an end-to-end understanding
6: Beyond the Core Classifier guidance • Noise schedules • Diffusion variants (SDEs, EDMs) • Open problems Explore advanced techniques and the current research frontier
